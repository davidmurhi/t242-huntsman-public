# Videos

| Video | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [controls_demo.mp4](controls_demo.mp4) (57 s) | The CAD-built MuJoCo model driven from the keyboard: walk, turn, strafe, the four gaits and the emergency stop. Bottom left shows the key presses; top right shows the telemetry the dashboard receives. |
| [terrain_slope.mp4](terrain_slope.mp4) (30 s) | Hand-written gait on a 15° slope in MuJoCo: 3.5 m in 30 s, walks up and down. |
| [terrain_mixed.mp4](terrain_mixed.mp4) (30 s) | Hand-written gait on mixed ground (slope, steps and rubble): 2.9 m, veers 24° and tilts up to 22°. |
| [terrain_steps.mp4](terrain_steps.mp4) (30 s) | Hand-written gait on 50 mm steps: 0.8 m, stalls at the first step. |
| [terrain_rubble.mp4](terrain_rubble.mp4) (30 s) | Hand-written gait on 80 mm rubble: 0.6 m, snags on rocks and veers 27°. These four runs are the baseline the learned controller must beat in Phase 2. |
| [kai_ppo_training_2M_steps.mp4](kai_ppo_training_2M_steps.mp4) (48 s) | Kai's PPO policy in PyBullet after 2 million training steps: the first learned controller that moves (it drifts left). |
| [kai_keyboard_driving.mp4](kai_keyboard_driving.mp4) (60 s) | Kai's PyBullet spider (version 6) driven from the keyboard through its gears. |
| [pybullet_walk.gif](../pybullet/pybullet_walk.gif) | The PyBullet placeholder spider walking and turning. |
| [huntsman_walk.mp4](../../../simulation/mujoco/media/huntsman_walk.mp4) | Short clip of the MuJoCo model walking. |

Kai's two recordings were re-encoded at 1280 px wide so they fit in the
repository. V Source's own videos of the physical robot are partner
